How does barrier SHAPE affect quantum tunneling probability?

🌟 Our Core Discovery & Visualization Goals

The Discovery: Textbooks usually only analyze plain rectangular barriers. Our computational research reveals that the geometric shape of a barrier drastically alters the quantum tunneling probability. In fact, an exponential barrier allows up to 9 million times more tunneling than a standard rectangular barrier of the exact same height and width! We defined the "Shape Factor" (S) to mathematically quantify this.

Visualization Goals: This web tool visualizes the WKB (Wentzel-Kramers-Brillouin) approximation integral across 6 different geometries dynamically. By mapping the decay rate inside the barrier, you can clearly see exactly why smooth slopes (like the Gaussian and Parabolic shapes) offer far less resistance to quantum particles than vertical walls.
