How does barrier SHAPE affect quantum tunneling probability?

🌟 Our Core Discovery & Visualization Goals

The Discovery: Textbooks usually only analyze plain rectangular barriers. Our computational research reveals that the geometric shape of a barrier drastically alters the quantum tunneling probability. Because transmission scales exponentially with the barrier's area, geometric shapes with lower average profiles (e.g., exponential, Gaussian) exhibit orders of magnitude higher transmission than a standard rectangular barrier of the exact same maximum height and width. This transmission ratio diverges exponentially as the barrier widens. We defined the "Shape Factor" (S) to mathematically quantify this shape-dependent blocking power.

Visualization Goals: This web tool visualizes the WKB (Wentzel-Kramers-Brillouin) approximation integral across 6 different geometries dynamically. By mapping the decay rate inside the barrier, you can clearly see exactly why smooth slopes (like the Gaussian and Parabolic shapes) offer far less resistance to quantum particles than vertical walls.
