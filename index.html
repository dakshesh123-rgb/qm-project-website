Numerical Investigation of Quantum Tunneling Through Non-Standard Barrier Geometries

Standard textbooks limit quantum tunneling to rectangular barriers due to analytical simplicity. This computational investigation demonstrates that barrier curvature alters transmission by up to 2.3 × 10⁶×, defines the universal WKB Shape Factor \(S\), and simulates wavepacket scattering live in your browser.

02 // Analytical Discovery

The WKB Geometric Shape Factor \((S)\)

Textbooks exclusively treat rectangular barriers. Under equal height \(V_0\) and width \(W\), curvature reduces the action integral \(\gamma = \int \sqrt{2m(V(x)-E)}dx\), causing massive exponential transmission amplification.
